Leaf EPUB reader and library manager

A calmer home for your books.

Your reading life, arranged like a shelf you actually want to spend time with.

Leaf is an EPUB reader and library manager for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Bring in your own books, sort them into shelves, track what you are reading, discover titles from catalogs, and move from one device to the next without losing the thread.

Bookish, not busy.

What Leaf does well

Features

The core idea is simple: make reading, organizing, and revisiting your books feel natural.

Read

EPUB reading with progress that follows you

Open EPUB books in a dedicated reader, keep your place, and settle into a reading flow that survives the jump between devices.

Organize

Shelves, series, ratings, and reading status

Arrange your collection the way readers actually think about books, from custom shelves to in-progress, finished, and someday-maybe piles.

Discover

Browse catalogs and import what you keep

Leaf supports OPDS catalog browsing alongside file imports, making it easy to grow your library from the sources you already use.

Sync

Keep your library close across devices

Cloud-backed syncing helps your reading life travel with you from iPhone to iPad to Mac, without turning the app into a science project.
