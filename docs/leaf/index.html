EPUB reader and library manager

A better place to keep your books.

Leaf brings reading and library management together in one warm, shelf-minded app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Import your EPUBs, organize them with shelves and series, browse OPDS catalogs, and pick up exactly where you left off on your next device.

Available for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Built for people who collect, sort, and actually read.

Some reading apps feel like file managers. Some library apps forget that books are meant to be opened. Leaf sits in the middle: a thoughtful place to keep your library in order without making the act of reading feel clinical.

What Leaf does well

Features

Four essentials for people who want their reading app to feel as considered as the books inside it.

Read

A reader that gets out of the way

Read EPUBs in a focused reader that remembers exactly where you left off.

Organize

A library arranged like one

Build shelves, track series, rate favorites, and see what is unread, underway, or finished.

Discover

Catalogs made for wandering

Browse OPDS catalogs and bring new finds straight into your library.

Sync

Your place, everywhere

Keep books and reading progress together across iPhone, iPad, and Mac with iCloud.
