About Leaf

A home for the books you bring with you.

Leaf is an EPUB reader and personal library for people who bring their own books. It pairs a customizable reading experience with shelves, series, reading statuses, OPDS Sources, and progress sync—all built around your books rather than a single store or subscription service.

Leaf can also sync reading progress through a KOReader sync server, making it possible to move between Leaf on iPhone or iPad and compatible e-ink readers without losing your place.

Leaf is available on iPhone and iPad, with Mac coming soon.

Features and Availability

Built around your library.

Read your own EPUBs

Give the EPUBs you already have a comfortable, focused place to read.

Make the page your own

Choose fonts, themes, spacing, and margins globally or book by book.

Keep the library tidy

Arrange books by shelf or series and track reading status and ratings.

Choose your Sources

Browse and import books from OPDS catalogs and Grimmory libraries, or add EPUBs directly from the Files app.

Continue across devices

Use iCloud to stay in sync across iPhone and iPad, and a KOReader sync server to continue reading between Leaf and compatible e-ink readers.

Available on the App Store

For iPhone and iPad, with Mac coming soon.
